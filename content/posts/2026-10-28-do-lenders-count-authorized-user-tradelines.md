> **Short answer:** It depends on the lender and the loan. Scoring models include authorized-user (AU) accounts, but with less weight than accounts you own in recent FICO versions. For mortgages, Fannie Mae's Desktop Underwriter takes AU tradelines into consideration, while manually underwritten loans generally cannot consider them unless the account belongs to another borrower or you document 12 months as the sole payer. Always answer lender questions truthfully.

## The score is not the whole decision

A credit score is a number built from your report. Underwriting is a separate decision that looks at the report itself, your income, debts, assets and the type of loan. An AU tradeline can lift the score and still be discounted by a human reviewer.

## What the mortgage rules say

**Manual underwriting.** Fannie Mae's Selling Guide says authorized-user tradelines cannot be considered in a manually underwritten decision unless:

- another borrower in the transaction owns the tradeline; or
- the borrower documents with canceled checks or payment receipts that they have been the actual and sole payer for at least 12 months before applying.

An AU tradeline must be considered if the owner is the borrower's spouse and not a borrower on the loan.

**Automated underwriting (DU).** Fannie Mae states that DU takes authorized-user tradelines into consideration as part of its credit risk assessment.

## The scoring-model landscape in late 2026

According to FHFA and Fannie Mae announcements:

- On **September 9, 2026**, Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac expanded VantageScore 4.0 to all approved lenders, without prior written approval.
- Lenders can still use Classic FICO. For manually underwritten loans, lenders must continue to use only Classic FICO.
- **FICO 10T** is approved as a model but is not currently eligible for loan delivery. The agencies say they will provide guidance when it is.
- FICO 10T uses trended data, so the balance pattern on an AU account may matter. A card with consistently low balances fits the behavior the model rewards.

## Credit cards and auto loans

Card and auto lenders use their own scorecards. Many use a FICO or VantageScore plus internal rules for income and existing debt, so an AU tradeline typically helps when it improves the inputs they care about: age, limit, utilization and payment history. There is no universal rule; the lender's policy decides.

## The truthfulness point

Appearing on a credit report as an authorized user is normal and legal. What matters is what you say on applications. If a lender asks about your accounts, income or the source of your credit history, answer accurately. An authorized-user account is not your own account, and applications that ask you to list "accounts you are responsible for" should be answered that way. Nothing here is legal advice; consult a qualified professional about your situation.

## How to use a tradeline responsibly

1. **Start early.** Allow the statement cycle, the posting window and a buffer. See [when tradelines post](/blog/when-do-tradelines-post-statement-dates/).
2. **Favor low-utilization, long-history cards.** $500 on a $10,000 limit is 5% utilization; $9,000 is 90%.
3. **Build your own accounts at the same time.** See [primary vs authorized-user tradelines](/blog/primary-vs-authorized-user-tradelines/).
4. **Check your three reports** before you apply, and ask your loan officer which score model and underwriting method they will use.
5. **Do not rely on any promise** of a particular score or approval. We do not guarantee outcomes.

## Frequently asked questions

### Will a mortgage lender ignore my authorized-user tradeline?
Possibly, in manual underwriting. Fannie Mae's guide generally excludes AU tradelines there unless specific conditions are met. In DU, they are considered.

### Does FICO 10T change the rules for authorized users?
Third-party analysis says FICO has not announced a different set of AU rules for 10T, but trended balance data may affect how much an AU account helps.

### Is VantageScore 4.0 accepted for mortgages now?
Yes. As of September 9, 2026, all Fannie Mae-approved lenders can use VantageScore 4.0 for eligible loans, though many still use Classic FICO.

### Will I be asked whether an account is mine?
Applications often ask about the accounts you are responsible for. Answer accurately.

### Should I wait to apply until the tradeline posts?
Usually yes. Verify it on your reports first, then apply.
