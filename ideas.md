# Design directions

1. **Passbook & Ledger** — warm ledger paper, ink-green, brass. Real-world cue: a bank passbook and a statement with ruled lines. Serif display (Fraunces), tabular figures, square corners, "statement-date stamp" on every card. Blog posts presented as ledger entries.
2. **Terminal Dark** — near-black dashboard, neon-green numerals, monospaced everything. Reads as fintech/crypto; poor fit for an education-first, trust-sensitive audience.
3. **Pastel Fintech** — soft gradients, rounded cards, illustrated 3D coins. Generic; what every credit-repair site already looks like.

**Chosen: 1, Passbook & Ledger.** It signals "records and accuracy", fits a blog-first site (long reading), and makes the one number that matters for tradelines, the statement date, a visual stamp on every product card.
Palette: paper #f3ecdc, ink #12261d, brass #c99a3b / #8a6418, rule #d9cfb8, alert #8c2b1f. Fonts: Fraunces (headings), Inter (UI), self-hosted woff2.
